# Same Shirt, Different Story

## A Short Guide to Persuasion, Branding, Design, and AI

How can one plain white T-shirt feel practical in one advertisement, adventurous in another, and rebellious in a third? This mini textbook explores how communication changes the meaning around a product while the product itself stays the same.

Written for first-year college students, the guide connects three lenses—persuasion, brand archetypes, and visual language—to a practical workflow for directing and reviewing AI-assisted work. No design or psychology background is required.

## How to Use This Guide

Read in order the first time. Each chapter builds vocabulary that the next one uses. Tables support comparison, and Mermaid diagrams show relationships; view the files on GitHub or in a Markdown preview that supports Mermaid.

Try the small exercises with a classmate. Describe what a presentation makes you expect, then check whether those expectations are supported. You can disagree with a campaign's direction and still explain how it works.

All T-shirt campaigns are imaginary classroom examples. They are not real advertisements, customer testimonials, or evidence of sales results. Historical and theoretical references appear beside the relevant claims. Sections labeled as external research suggestions give starting points for your own investigation.

Before accepting or committing AI-generated work, compare it with the assigned issue, verify factual claims, inspect changes, and decide what to revise. The student remains responsible for the final editorial judgment.
